This second simulation features one of, if not the worst designed intersection in all of Craiova. This is a simplified version of the intersection between Bulevardul Dacia and Strada Amaradia, which joins together 4 major traffic flows, resulting in a never ending traffic jams. The points of interest featured are 
- CNP Stefan Velovan
- The main road leading to Craiovita
- Rocada
- The north exit of the city
